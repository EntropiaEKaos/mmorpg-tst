# Mor'ia 9.28–9.29 — Terrain Materials & Character Readability

## 9.28 Terrain / environment
- irregular multi-tone city cobbles replace the repeating brick carpet;
- cracks, moss seams and randomized stone widths create material variation;
- buildings gain directional cast shadows and a harder grounding/contact edge;
- environment depth remains presentation-only.

## 9.29 Characters / creatures
- authored humanoid sprite scale increases from 1.30 to 1.42 for stronger world readability;
- pixel glints preserve crisp material separation at the larger silhouette;
- dragons/wyrms, ghosts/wraiths, demons and lich/necromancers now have dedicated procedural silhouettes;
- elite/boss aura and contact-shadow language from 9.27 remains intact.
