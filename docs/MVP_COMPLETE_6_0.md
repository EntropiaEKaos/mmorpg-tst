# MOR'IA MVP Complete 6.0

This release promotes previously browser-only features into the authoritative online runtime.

## Official online systems

- Dungeons with private server-owned waves and rewards
- Pets/companions with server-owned purchases and combat assist
- Depot and banking
- Mail with server-owned gold attachments
- Auction House with escrow and seller credit
- Mor'ia Coins earned and spent server-side
- Rotating World Events driven by ContentDB/default templates
- NPC services: shop, food, rest and capped training
- Crafting
- Gem socketing and equipment set bonuses
- Bestiary and achievements
- Daily rewards and stamina
- Gathering and professions
- Books and server-validated Mystery quests
- Weapon mastery
- Opt-in PvP with authoritative damage and skull escalation

The legacy browser implementations remain available in Quick Play/offline mode, but authenticated online characters use only server-owned state and economy for these systems.

Maps remain intentionally static in `World.mjs`; the Admin Maps tab is still a reference catalog until a real terrain/portal editor is implemented.
