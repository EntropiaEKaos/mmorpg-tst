# Mor'ia 9.30–9.32 — Combat VFX, City Lighting & HUD Polish

## 9.30 Combat VFX
Authoritative combat events now select layered presentation recipes by elemental school. Damage and spell events receive core/accent/spark bursts; critical hits increase presentation shake; elemental reactions gain recognizable cross-school bursts. Damage formulas remain server-owned.

## 9.31 City lighting
Lamps, braziers and crystals now emit local radial light in the existing presentation layer, improving nighttime city readability without creating collision or gameplay state.

## 9.32 HUD polish
The obsidian/brass skin gains consistent hover and keyboard-focus states, richer slot depth, custom scrollbars and explicit reduced-motion behavior.
